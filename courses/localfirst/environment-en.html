Closed environment

Establish operational capability without external connectivity first.

Physical and logical isolation

Physical isolation removes external connectivity. Logical isolation relies on segmentation and traffic rules, for example. They are not equivalent.

Local dependencies

Identify DNS, time, authentication, software, storage and backups. Test startup and recovery without internet.
